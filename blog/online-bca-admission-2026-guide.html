August 10, 2026 · 7 min read · onlinebca.in

Online BCA Admission 2026 — Quick Overview

Online BCA (Bachelor of Computer Applications) admissions for 2026 are open at multiple NAAC-accredited universities. No entrance exam is required by most universities. Eligibility is typically Class 12 pass from any stream. Applications are accepted year-round with rolling intakes (July and January batches). Total fee ₹80K–₹1.35L over 3 years.

Documents Required for Online BCA Admission

  • Class 10 marksheet and certificate
  • Class 12 marksheet and certificate
  • ID proof: Aadhaar card / Passport / Voter ID
  • Passport-size photographs (3-4)
  • Category certificate (SC/ST/OBC if applicable)
  • Migration certificate (if from different state board)

Online BCA Admission Process — Step by Step

  • Step 1: Choose university and apply online via official portal or through Jaro Education
  • Step 2: Submit documents — scan and upload (digital process for most universities)
  • Step 3: Application review — typically 2-5 working days
  • Step 4: Receive admission confirmation and fee invoice
  • Step 5: Pay first year fee (EMI options available)
  • Step 6: Get enrollment number and LMS (Learning Management System) access
  • Step 7: Attend orientation and first live session

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the last date for online BCA admission 2026?

Most universities have rolling admissions for online BCA — you can apply any time. July 2026 batch closes around June-July 2026; January 2027 batch closes around December 2026. Apply early to get your preferred batch and avoid last-minute document delays.

Can I do online BCA after 12th arts?

Yes — online BCA admits students from all streams: Science, Commerce, and Arts. You do not need PCM in Class 12 to pursue BCA. The BCA curriculum starts with programming fundamentals — no prior coding knowledge is required.

What is the fee for online BCA in EMI?

Monthly EMI for online BCA: Manipal MUJ (~₹80K over 3 years = ~₹2,200/month on 36-month EMI), Amrita (~₹1.35L = ~₹3,750/month on 36-month EMI), LPU (~₹1.2L = ~₹3,330/month). No-cost (zero interest) EMI is available through partner banks and NBFCs for most programmes.
